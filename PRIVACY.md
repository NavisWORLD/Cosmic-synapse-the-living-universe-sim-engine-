# Privacy

SIM EARTH 7.07 is designed as a local-first simulation.

The game may request microphone, camera, geolocation, device orientation, or motion access. Permission is optional. The canonical standalone build processes those browser signals locally for simulation/state input and does not require a project account or remote telemetry service.

World persistence uses browser/device storage. Clearing browser/app data can remove saved worlds. Third-party browsers, operating systems, hosting providers, app stores, or wrapper runtimes may have their own privacy behavior outside this project's code.

Teachers and organizations should not require camera, microphone, or precise location unless they have an appropriate policy and consent basis.
