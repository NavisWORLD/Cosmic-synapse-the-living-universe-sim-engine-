## q4_24 - The Forgotten
**Location:** Whispering Woods Ruins and Altar


### Beat b1
*Setting:* Ruins in the Whispering Woods. Two great statues with vines, eyes carved shut. Murals pulse blue.

*Action:* The statues speak in one voice. A dialogue choice; every honest one works.

- **Statues of Eldoria:** State your purpose.
- **Arin:** To help the people here.
- **Statues of Eldoria:** ...honest. Enter.

### Beat b2
*Setting:* Old altar in a sunken ring of stone. A wraith with dark grasping tendrils, fed on fear for centuries.

*Action:* The Forgotten rises. Low Light makes it hit harder.

- **The Forgotten:** Your fear smells sweet, / little lantern.
- **Arin:** I believe in the power of / hope.
- **Zeref:** *puffs up to twice his / size*

### Beat b3
*Setting:* A glowing crystal rises from the altar. Its beam punches through the ruins into the sky.

*Action:* The land blooms. The Guardians of Eldoria wake and thank them.

- **Guardian of Eldoria:** Long we slept. Thank you, / Still-Here one.
- **Arin:** The crops?
- **Guardian of Eldoria:** Already turning green.

### Dungeon: Whispering Woods Ruins (Whispering Woods / Ruined Citadels)
- **r1** Statue gate, carved murals pulse faint blue. Enemies: Guardian Statue. Puzzle: Answer the statues honestly; then walk the safe mural lane. Loot: herb_poultice
- **r2** Collapsed hall, trap floors under moss. Enemies: Shade. Puzzle: Crystal Lantern reveals the true floor; fake tiles drop. Loot: lamp_oil, ink_pot
- **r3** Fear corridor: whispers make shadows longer. Enemies: Fear Tendril. Puzzle: Keep Light above half or tendrils double. Loot: healing_herbs
- **r4** The old altar in a sunken stone ring. Enemies: none. Puzzle: Boss room. Loot: calaminth

**Encounter: The Forgotten** - _bible boss #10 (Whispering Woods ruins, Act IV, Heart 5,500, 3 phases; fear-fed wraith; finisher Crystal Lantern beam)_

Mechanic: Your fear makes it stronger: low Light means it hits harder. Keep Light up; finish with the Crystal Lantern beam.
- Idle: 4f, Ragged wraith sways over the altar, tendrils curling like smoke ink.
- Phase 1 (100-65%): A wraith twice Arin's height, a face that is mostly hollow.
  - Grasping Tendrils: tell 'Ground shadows twitch', 3 tendrils grab; mash to escape (4f: Twitch, rise, grab, pull)
  - Fear Whisper: tell 'Text goes gray, it leans in', Drains Light (3f: Lean, whisper, retreat)
- Phase 2 (65-30%): It feeds: swells to fill the altar ring, tendrils climb every pillar.
  - Pillar Grab: tell 'Pillars darken top to bottom', Tendrils drop from above (5f: Darken, drip, drop, grab, lift)
  - Nightmare Bloom: tell 'Body opens like a dark flower', Arena dims, it hits harder (4f: Open, bloom, dim, close)
- Phase 3 (30-0%): A dark storm the size of the ruins, a thousand faces of old fears in it.
  - Centuries of Fear: tell 'Every face screams silently', Wave of tendrils fills the floor (6f: Faces, scream, wave, wave, wave, recede)
  - Eclipse: tell 'Lantern light flickers', Lantern dims; hold beam to answer (5f: Flicker, dim, dark, charge, burst)
- Defeat: Lantern beam pierces it, faces fade one by one, smoke unravels, altar glows, crystal rises, birds. "...hope... burns..."

**Zeref moment:** Zeref puffs up at a wraith 100 times his size. Wins.

**Rewards:** heart_eldoria
