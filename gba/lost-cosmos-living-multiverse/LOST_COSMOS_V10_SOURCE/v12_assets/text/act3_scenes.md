- **Mote:** hmmmm

### Beat b3

- **Spirit of Harmony:** Remember. You are never / alone.

- **r1** Thorn tunnel, gaps open and close like slow teeth. Enemies: Stillborn Thornling. Puzzle: Walk only while the breath ring is on 'out'; thorns relax then. Loot: healing_herbs
- **r2** Bramble maze around three dim rune stones. Enemies: Shade Wisp. Puzzle: Point at each rune in order: protection, growth, illumination. Loot: calaminth, grove_honey
- **r3** Heart of the grove: a tangle with twelve hand-shaped knots. Enemies: none. Puzzle: Boss room. Each freed villager joins the circle. Loot: cinnamon

**Encounter: The Grove in Chaos (Thorn Tangle)** - _bible 3.2 Grove in Chaos (12-person Attunement breath); grove encounter, Attunable, not a bible numbered boss_

Mechanic: Not a fight to win by hitting. Free villagers from thorns, then hold the 12-person breath ring to unwind the tangle.
- Idle: 4f, Brambles creak inward and out, slow as a sleeper's chest.
- Phase 1 (100-60%): A knot of thorns the size of a house, twelve villagers stuck in its hatching.
  - Thorn Lash: tell 'A bramble pulls back, creaking', Line whip, knockback (3f: Pull back, whip, retract)
  - Bramble Wall: tell 'Ground ink-scribbles in a line', Wall cuts the arena in half (4f: Scribble, sprout, thicken, hold)
- Phase 2 (60-25%): The tangle grows up the trees and over the sky; the page darkens.
  - Thorn Rain: tell 'Canopy shakes, dust drifts down', Thorns fall in 3 lanes (4f: Shake, drop, drop, stick)
  - Choke Ring: tell 'Ring of thorns forms around the circle', Breath ring shrinks; keep rhythm (5f: Rise, close, tighten, pulse, hold)
- Phase 3 (25-0%): Every vine rears at once into a giant thorn-face with hollow eyes.
  - Scream of Brambles: tell 'Face opens, all sound drops', Breath rhythm speeds up 2x (4f: Open, roar lines, shake, settle)
- Defeat: Vines unwind, petals open, gold pollen rises, a bee flies out, grove breathes. "...ahhh."
- Sprite note: Uses biome bramble tiles; no new big sprite needed beyond the thorn-face.

**Zeref moment:** Zeref exhales at last. The whole grove smells of honey.

**Rewards:** grove_honey
