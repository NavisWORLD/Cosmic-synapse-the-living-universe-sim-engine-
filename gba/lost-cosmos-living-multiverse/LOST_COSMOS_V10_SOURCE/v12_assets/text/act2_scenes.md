- **Forest Guardian:** Your test is below. In the / cave. Something waits / there, and it is afraid.

### Dungeon: The Wisp Trail (Whispering Woods)
- **r1** Bent trail of moss stones; wisps hop ahead. Enemies: Moth Swarm. Puzzle: Step only on stones a wisp just lit; others sink into ink. Loot: healing_herbs
- **r2** Hollow log tunnel, roots twitch as you pass. Enemies: Root Grabber. Puzzle: Mote's glow scares roots back; keep Mote near to pass. Loot: glowcap, grove_honey

**Encounter: The Forest Guardian (Test of Courage)** - _bible 2.3 (ancient guardian, three tests); test encounter, Attunable, not a bible numbered boss_

- Phase 1 (100-60%): Ancient guardian of ink branches, antlered, wisps in its crown.
  - Antler Charge: tell 'Lowers head, hoof scrapes 2x, dust puff', Line dash, knockback (4f: Head down, scrape, lunge, skid stop)
  - Leaf Storm: tell 'Antlers shake, leaves rise', 3 slow leaf projectiles (3f: Shake, leaves lift, fling)
- Phase 2 (60-0%): Antlers grow twice as wide, wisps caught in them glow like eyes.
  - Wisp Snare: tell 'Caught wisps flash red', Wisps fire at your last spot (4f: Antler glow, wisps detach, streak, pop)
  - Double Charge: tell 'Two hoof scrapes and a snort ring', Two dashes back to back (5f: Lower, lunge, turn, lunge, pant)
- Defeat: Kneels, antlers drop leaves, bows, steps aside for the next test. "You did not run. Pass."

## q2_04 - Grumbleback
**Location:** The Hollow Cave

*Action:* Arin enters alone, except for the beasts, who refuse to be left.

- **Arin:** The Guardian said alone.

- **Grumbleback:** GRRRRMMMMMM
- **Mote:** hmmmm rawr hmmm

### Beat b3
*Setting:* Same chamber, crystal light flooding. Ghostly ink hands painted over every wall.

*Action:* Arin touches the crystal: visions of the First Hands. Grumbleback blocks the exit.

- **Arin:** I saw them. The ones who / made all this.
- **Grumbleback:** *sits on the exit*
- **Arin:** Are you... coming with us?
- **Grumbleback:** *sits on Arin*

### Dungeon: The Hollow Cave (Hollow Groves)
- **r1** Crystal stalactites drip light; floor tiles crack. Enemies: Crystal Bat. Puzzle: Trap floor: cracked tiles fall after 1 sec. Follow the dry tiles. Loot: cave_moss
- **r2** Narrow gallery of beast nests and bones. Enemies: Shard Crawler, Crystal Bat. Puzzle: Push glow crystals into sockets to light the gallery. Loot: golem_grease, golden_wheat
- **r3** Echo pool. Your reflection moves late. Enemies: none. Puzzle: Hum at the pool: the echo opens a hidden crawlspace. Loot: crystal_lantern
- **r4** Grumbleback's den: moss bed, a pile of stolen socks. Enemies: none. Puzzle: Boss room. Loot: wet_sock

**Encounter: Grumbleback, the Cave Beast**

Mechanic: Attack it and Agitation rises; Block and Hum to drop Agitation. Attune at 0 to win kindly.
- Phase 1 (100-70%): Half hidden in dark, only eye and claws visible.
  - Dark Lunge: tell 'Eye glows, moss bristles', Lunge from shadow, big damage (4f: Eye flash, crouch, leap, land with dust)
  - Rock Swipe: tell 'Arm pulls back, dust falls', Wide arc swipe (3f: Wind up, swipe, follow through)
- Phase 2 (70-35%): Stands fully: twice as tall, crystals sprout on its back.
  - Crystal Quake: tell 'Rears up on hind legs, crystals glow', Falling stalactites in 3 lanes (5f: Rear, hang, slam, ripple, stalactites fall)
  - Roar: tell 'Mouth opens, sound lines', Pushback, cancels Hum (3f: Inhale, roar rings, close)
- Phase 3 (35-0%): Back crystals blaze, moss lifts like a mane. Scared, not angry.
  - Panic Roll: tell 'Curls into a ball, crystals spin', Bounces around arena 3 times (6f: Curl, spin, bounce, bounce, bounce, dizzy)
  - Sad Howl: tell 'Looks at its socks, eyes wet', Slows party; Hum here Attunes instantly (4f: Look down, sniff, howl up, sniffle)
- Defeat: Flops on its back, rolls over, nuzzles Arin, tiny butterfly lands on nose, it sneezes. "Grrmm. (you can stay)"

**Zeref moment:** Zeref climbs Grumbleback and sits on his head like a crown.

**Rewards:** sacred_crystal, grumble_plush

- **r2** Spiral of trees closing tighter. Enemies: Dust Moth. Puzzle: Walk the spiral without letting the lantern die. Loot: flint_wick

  - Eye Flash: tell 'All eye spots open', Fear: input reverse 2s (3f: Close, open all eyes, flash)

## q2_07 - The Sentinel
**Location:** Citadel Atrium


### Beat b1
*Setting:* Citadel atrium: pillars lost in black. A cold wind visibly sweeps across the frame.

*Action:* Every lantern goes out. Only Mote and Zeref glow.

- **Quillan:** My soup went cold. / Instantly. That's illegal.
- **Mote:** *glows brighter* rawr

### Beat b2
*Setting:* A hooded dark figure on a stair. Only the glow from the beasts outlines her.

*Action:* Choose Reason or Fight. Fight is short; she just waits.

- **Dark Figure:** Leave. This place eats the / lost.
- **Arin:** We aren't lost. We are / still here.
- **Dark Figure:** ...everyone says that.

### Beat b3
*Setting:* Hood falls back: woman with snow-white wings, piercing eyes. Lanterns relight blue.

*Action:* Hesper descends. A butterfly lands on her wing; she fails to stay serious.

- **Sentinel Hesper:** I am Hesper. I will guide / you through the trials.
- **Sentinel Hesper:** ...is there a butterfly on / me?
- **Zeref:** *pounces on wing*

### Dungeon: The Lightless Atrium (Ruined Citadels)
- **r1** Pitch black hall; only glow radius visible. Enemies: Shade Wisp. Puzzle: Keep the beasts close; glow radius is the map. Loot: sunseed_snack
- **r2** Stair of forgotten candles. Enemies: none. Puzzle: Relight candles with Mote's sneeze to reveal the stairs. Loot: star_shard

**Encounter: Sentinel Hesper (Reason or Fight)**

Mechanic: She never truly attacks; she only waits. Choosing Reason ends it. Fighting just tires you.
- Phase 1 (100-50%): Hooded shape in the dark.
  - Cold Wind: tell 'Cloak flares out', Pushes you back, dims glow (3f: Cloak lifts, gust lines, settles)
  - Wing Guard: tell 'Wings rise under cloak', Blocks all hits 3s (3f: Wings lift, fold over, hold)
- Phase 2 (50-0%): Cloak tears; white wings spread full screen width.
  - Feather Wall: tell 'Wings spread wide, feathers glint', Ring of feathers stops you, no damage (5f: Spread, glint, feathers fly, ring, fade)
  - Patient Stare: tell 'She sits down', Nothing. She waits for you to stop. (2f: Sits, blinks)
- Defeat: Hood falls, she smiles, butterfly lands, she sneezes softly. "Enough. Walk with me."

**Zeref moment:** In the dark, Zeref's eyes are the last stars. He blinks.

**Rewards:** sentinel_down
