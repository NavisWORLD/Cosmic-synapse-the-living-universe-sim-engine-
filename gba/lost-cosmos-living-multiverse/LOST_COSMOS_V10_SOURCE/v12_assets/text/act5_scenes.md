**Encounter: Malakar, the Unflowing** - _bible boss #13 (The Stilled Sea, Act V, 4 phases per bible Section 9; Attunable in Phase 4; final boss)_

Mechanic: P1 fold the arena, Draw Mode bridges. P2 perfect light, Dusk Walk to the seam and strike his shadow. P3 Arin freezes, control Mote alone. P4 Attune, Strike or Rest.
- Idle: 4f, Stands calm by the frozen sea, maps rustling under his arm, cloak never moving.
- Phase 1 (100-70%): The Cartographer: a tall calm man; the whole beach creases and folds like his map.
  - Compass Needles: tell 'Raises a compass, needles spin', 3 needles fly at you (4f: Raise, spin, aim, throw)
  - Fold: tell 'The ground creases with a line', Platforms flip; Draw Mode bridges (5f: Crease, lift, fold, flip, flat)

- Phase 2 (70-40%): The Counterfeit: he becomes perfect white light, sky-high, beautiful, shadowless, untouchable.
  - Offer of Rest: tell 'Screen softens, colors brighten', Fills Stilling; Dusk Walk to the seam (5f: Soften, brighten, glow, hush, hold)
  - Perfect Light: tell 'He opens his arms wide', White beam freezes what it hits (4f: Open, gather, beam, fade)
- Phase 3 (40-0%): The Unflowing: a gigantic tide of stillness. Arin freezes into a drawing; the Mote crosses alone.
  - Stilled Wave: tell 'A frozen wave rises over the horizon', Wave crushes a lane; Mote dodges (6f: Rise, tower, tip, fall, freeze, crack)
  - Total Still: tell 'The sea goes silent, all color drains', Mote slows; use Echoes, keep moving (5f: Silence, drain, slow, slow, spark)
- Phase 4 (none): The Empty Seat: not a fight. Malakar sits small in an old two-seat cart; the seat is empty.
  - The Slowest Breath: tell 'His chest rises once, very slow', Attune: match a 1-minute breath (6f: In, hold, out, in, hold, out)
- Defeat: Attune: tears fall, one wave moves, then another, sea flows, he walks off, lets a crab pass. "...that one. Yes."
- Endings: end_a, end_b, end_c, end_d
- Sprite note: P3: Mote's Spark fires at Ink stage (scripted if not); Arin stands with 1 Heart; a butterfly lands on Malakar. P4 Attune -> end_a/end_d, Strike -> end_b, Rest -> end_c.
