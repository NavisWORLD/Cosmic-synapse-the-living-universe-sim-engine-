# Lost Cosmos V11.3 Act I

Act I is added on top of the V11.2 Spark cartridge. The eight-signal road, Spark Beasts, Charlet, the twelve rares, Beast Box (LCX1, LCR1, LCM1), and the 10-card prologue stay. The ROM header title remains `COSMOS V11.2` so existing emulator saves still attach.

## Playable now

- Intro on the first step into a V11 region: the lattice is a synapse, and the listener is the person who can hear it.
- A listener in every region (lantern on the west shelf) with a short line for that nerve of the road.
- Lys, a rival on Eridoria Prime. Talk once, then press A again on her mark to battle her Skyspark. Winning shares the road.
- Flower patches hold wild sparks. Stand on a flower and press A. The battle is turn-based: Fight (four moves), Befriend, Potion, Run. Types follow the existing Ember, Tide, and Bloom cycle. Void and Neutral are even.
- Befriend a spark once it is at 40% HP or lower. It joins with bond 55. Evolution is unchanged: level 12 and bond 55, then level 28 and bond 80. Open the party to evolve.
- The field HUD is one line: region, level, an HP bar. The bottom row is the current Act I goal or the tracked quest, plus the tile action and START.
- Chapter goals rise as you befriend a spark, beat Lys, light beacons, face the Quiet, and hear the crown listener after the Quiet rests. That last talk is the end of Act I.
- Story progress is saved in previously unused LC11 bytes 648–653, inside the existing CRC. A V11.2 save (those bytes zero) loads and the act catches up to beacons and bosses already earned.

## Windows and motion

Dialogue, the transit card, the field bars, spark battles, the journal, pause, shops, and the Beast Box use one navy window: a cyan frame, a blank row under the title, and text kept in columns 2–27. Field sprites stay off while a box is open. Spark Beast portraits are drawn from spare sprite tiles so they no longer overwrite the species art. In a spark battle the panel is opaque every frame. Creatures idle-bob and blink from their seed, lunge on a hit, shake when hurt, bounce when befriended, and pulse when they evolve. Evolution still asks for level 12 and bond 55, then level 28 and bond 80.

## Whole-frame present (flicker fix)

The game used to clear and redraw the UI map, OAM, and palettes straight into VRAM while the screen was scanning out. Busy frames ran past vblank, so menus flickered, the map page showed half-drawn frames, and palette writes bled into the visible picture. Now each frame is drawn into IWRAM shadows (UI map, OAM, BG/OBJ palettes, and the display/BG registers). `lc_present()` copies them in one burst right after vblank starts, so a frame is either the old one or the new one, never a mix. Render runs before the vsync wait. Host QA builds are unchanged.

Text keeps a blank tile of padding inside each edge. The prologue and epilogue header sits on row 1 of a three-row band. The box title has a blank row above it, the body lines have a blank row between them (up to three lines, so no card is cut short), and the art is raised 12 px to stay in view. Tabbed pause pages lower the UI layer 4 px so the tab strip no longer touches the top of the screen. Text fits its box: the title hints sit on row 18 above the frame, long battle notices use three rows when the command list leaves room, and clipped hints on the map and bestiary pages are shorter. The spark battle portraits and the duel hero sprite no longer cover the name, HP, or stat rows.

## Lys

Lys is tuned so a Level 2 player can win with a potion: her Skyspark has 30 + 4 x level HP, and her retaliation bonus is +4.

## Music and sound

All music is original and lives in `tools/lc_chiptune.py`, which writes `LOST_COSMOS_V10_SOURCE/lc_music_data.h`. The sequencer in `lc_audio.h` uses only the DMG PSG: square 1 for the lead, the wave channel for a triangle bass, and noise for drums. Square 2 (with noise when needed) plays sound effects. It costs one table step per tick, with no DMA or mixing.

| Track | Where | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lattice Dawn | title, prologue | D major |
| Synapse Road | overworld | G major |
| Spark Clash | wild sparks, duels | E minor |
| Skyspark Rival | Lys battle | A minor, raised 7th |
| Signal Kept | victory jingle | one shot, then the field track |
| Held Breath | the Quiet, Crown, Act I end, credits | D dorian, no drums |

Sound effects: menu move, confirm, cancel, attack, hit, critical, befriend, potion, run, level up, and a creature cry. Each spark's cry and attack pitch and length are shifted by its seed bytes.

OPTIONS on the title screen has SOUND (on/off), MUSIC, and SFX (OFF, LOW, MID, HIGH). SYSTEM in the pause menu has the same MUSIC and SFX rows after the existing ones. The levels share SRAM byte 15 with the old audio flag. When both levels are HIGH, the byte stays 0 or 1, the same as V11.2. Other levels use `0x80 | on<<4 | music | sfx<<2`. A V11.2 save loads with both levels at HIGH.

## Story walkthrough test

`tests/test_mgba_story_act1.py` plays Act I with the controller only. It covers the intro, the Listener, a flower spark (Run, Befriend refused while strong, Fight, Potion, Befriend), rest, Lys (talk, battle, win), all eight signals and bosses, the Quiet, and the crown listener. It ends with a cold boot and CONTINUE. `--record clip.mp4` records the opening and the Quiet with sound.

## Still the same

Duels, shops, caches, camps, beacons, crafting, the Beast Box mailbox, Charlet, the twelve rares, and the original five-act campaign are still in the ROM.

## Planned after Act I

A separate overworld, random step encounters, a new duel screen, automatic evolution, species beyond the eight sparks, a rewritten prologue, Act II, and any change to the LCX1 layout are not in this upgrade.
