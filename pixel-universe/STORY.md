# THE LOST COSMOS — Story Bible

## Premise

The old SIM EARTH universe did not die. It lost depth.

A failed archival event flattened the living simulation into disconnected planes. The X, Y, and Z axes still exist as mathematical state, but the keys that bind them into one navigable world were scattered into three surviving biomes. The player wakes inside the recovered Synapse archive with one companion: **COSMOS**, a small autonomous simulated agent reconstructed from persistent state and a frozen quantum-derived tape.

COSMOS does not know the whole story. It remembers places only after visiting them. It can select destinations without the player ordering it to, can become cautious around the Black Garden, can become more trusting as keys are recovered, and can choose the first proposed ending at the Synapse Crown.

## Chapters

### I — The World That Lost Depth
The player wakes near Origin Earth. COSMOS reports that the universe still has coordinates but no stable agreement between them. Three Axis Keys are broadcasting.

### II — X / Ember Axis
The X key is buried in a furnace world whose rails point forever toward an absent horizon. Recovering it stabilizes horizontal distance.

### III — Y / Tide Memory
The Y key is stored in an ocean archive. Reflections contain fragments of deleted maps. Recovering it stabilizes relational distance: near/far, before/after, self/other.

### IV — Z / Bloom Z
The Z key lives in a vertical forest that grew when the universe forgot which way was up. Recovering it restores depth and reveals the Synapse Crown.

### The Black Garden
Optional. A gravity wound full of discarded future states. COSMOS dislikes it but is curious. Visiting it permanently enters the place into COSMOS memory.

### V — The Synapse Crown
With all three keys, the Crown accepts a state fusion. COSMOS chooses the first proposal from the quantum tape:

- **OPEN** — allow new procedural worlds to write themselves into the map.
- **PRESERVE** — freeze the recovered universe as a protected archive.
- **WANDER** — leave the Crown asleep and keep exploring beyond the authored story.

The player can continue flying after any ending. The sandbox remains the final chapter.
