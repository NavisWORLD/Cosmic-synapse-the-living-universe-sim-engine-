How your beast gets into the game

This page rebuilds your beast from its saved recipe: its three traits, its recorded IBM quantum run, and the keeper name. It then writes the same 32 KB starter battery that Spark Gate and the Beast Box dock use. That battery has the LCX1 mailbox with the beast's BCP1 profile, its LCG1 growth record, and SPK1 pixel art for all three forms. Lost Cosmos V11.2 reads it when you choose NEW GAME and adds the beast to your party as your companion. Open START, then PARTY, to see it.

Limits: the cartridge unlocks native forms at its own level and bond gates, so web training does not skip in-game evolution. One companion enters per cartridge run, so reload this page to bring a different beast. If this device already holds a journey for this beast, the handheld resumes it and you choose CONTINUE. Journeys stay in this browser. Use "Save journey .sav" in the handheld to keep a copy.

These buttons sit outside the screen and work once the cartridge is running. Keyboard: arrows, Z = A, X = B, Enter = START, V = SELECT, Q / E = L / R.

Lost Cosmos is original homebrew in an isolated emulator. Quantum data is recorded historical IBM Quantum counts used as fixed seeds. There is no live quantum link. Beasts are game companions, not conscious beings. Nothing is uploaded.
