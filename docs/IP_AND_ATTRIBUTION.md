# IP & Attribution Guide

## What the repository license does
Apache-2.0 gives broad rights to use, modify, and distribute the software while requiring preservation of the license and relevant NOTICE attribution. It also contains explicit patent-license language for contributor patent claims necessarily infringed by contributions.

## What it does not do
An open-source license does not create a patent, register a trademark, prove inventorship, or stop independent development of an idea. Public disclosure can affect patent strategy depending on jurisdiction and timing.

## Practical provenance steps
- preserve Git commit history;
- make signed/tagged releases when practical;
- archive releases to Zenodo and record DOI/version hashes;
- keep a clear `NOTICE`, `CITATION.cff`, and author list;
- mark third-party code/assets and their licenses;
- keep research claims dated and versioned.
