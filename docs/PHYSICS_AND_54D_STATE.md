## Planetary equations used as engineering references
For a world of mass `M` and radius `R`:

- surface gravity: `g = G M / R²`
- escape velocity: `v_escape = sqrt(2 G M / R)`
- mean density: `rho = M / ((4/3) pi R³)`

For approximate atmospheric scale height:

- `H = R_gas T / (M_molar g)`

For a near-circular solar orbit at semi-major axis `a`:

- `v_orbit = sqrt(G M_sun / a)`

The game normalizes several physical quantities before state fusion so large SI ranges do not dominate the adaptive channels.

## Lorenz component
A bounded numerical Lorenz update supplies three coupled chaotic-state channels. It is used as a dynamical control/input texture and should not be interpreted as a literal hidden force acting on the planet.

## Golden-ratio / Φ channels
Φ-based mappings are retained as part of CST lineage and as deterministic nonlinear feature transforms. Their inclusion is a model design choice and research hypothesis, not established evidence that the golden ratio is a universal optimizer of information processing.
